Why West Michigan

Why investors look at West Michigan

Kent, Ottawa, Allegan and Muskegon counties offer a mix of city neighborhoods, growing suburbs and lakeshore towns. Here’s how we think about the market — honestly.

Small multifamily is the sweet spot

Older 2–4 unit buildings in city neighborhoods are where the numbers most often work. Single-family homes at today’s median prices rarely cash flow with financing.

Local rules matter

Michigan’s tax uncapping, the non-homestead rate, and city rental certification all change the math. We build them in so there are no surprises after closing.

Speed wins

Deals that pass are rare and go fast. Delcie watches new listings, price drops and back-on-market properties so you can act the same day.
